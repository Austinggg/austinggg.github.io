My research asks how LLM-based agents can help people carry out knowledge-intensive tasks. I am particularly interested in the connection between workflow design and effective human–AI collaboration.

## Agent workflows and multi-agent coordination

I study the organization of agents, tools, and resources into workflows for complex tasks. My interests include workflow orchestration and coordination between agents.

## Human–AI interaction and alignment

I am interested in how agents can work with people throughout a task, and how their behavior can align with human goals. This includes the role of human interaction in agent-driven research workflows.

## Scientific automation

My broader goal is to support scientific work through effective human–AI interaction. I explore agent workflows for research tasks and the ways people can guide and assess them.

## Earlier research

My previous research experience includes medical AI, knowledge graphs, recommendation algorithms, and blockchain systems. These areas inform my current interest in agents that combine knowledge and reasoning.
